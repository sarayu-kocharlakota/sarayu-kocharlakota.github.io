1 — Pen Color10-color palette lets you draw in any color. Each stroke can be a different hue — great for color-coding problems or highlighting key vectors.
2 — Stroke SizeDrag the stroke slider to control line thickness from hairline to bold. Thicker strokes for diagrams, finer for detailed equations.
3 — Writing OpacitySet opacity from 0–100%. Lower opacity for ghost annotations or layered diagrams without obscuring the board.
4 — Highlighter Color3 highlighter colors (yellow, red, green). Red is the AI trigger — highlight anything red and Echo solves it instantly. Yellow and green for manual annotation.
5 — Board StyleSwitch between Chalkboard (dark green), Grid (white + lines), and Paper (plain white) to match your learning style or subject.
6 — Echo AI ChatbotEcho is the real-time AI engine. It reads your highlighted drawings, solves equations, explains diagrams, and can even draw answers back onto the board.
7 — Clear Board & Share"Clear board" wipes the canvas for a fresh start. "Share" exports the full session — drawings, solutions, and all — so students can save their work.

Home Screen — Track Your Feelings
